Good questions make for better decisions.

Do I need to connect my email account?+

No. Paste the email you want to check or upload a plain-text .eml or .txt file. Postmark doesn’t ask for access to your inbox.

What does the risk score mean?+

The score is the higher of the trained model’s rounded spam estimate and supplemental security points, capped at 100. Scores of 0–19 are labeled likely safe, 20–49 suspicious, and 50–100 likely spam. The final score is not a calibrated probability or a measure of model accuracy.

Who can see my analysis history?+

Your saved analyses, statistics, and exports are linked to your signed-in account. Other users cannot access them. Email content is stored in the app’s database so you can revisit your results.

Can Postmark tell if an email is true?+

Postmark checks for common spam and suspicious-content signals. It cannot verify factual claims, authenticate a sender, or guarantee that a message is safe. It does not open links or scan attachments.
